Questionnaire 5.0.2 supporting Moodle 5.0, 5.1 and 5.2 has been released. Details are in the ["CHANGES" file](https://github.com/PoetOS/moodle-mod_questionnaire/blob/v5.0.2/CHANGES.md). The release can be found on [Github](https://github.com/PoetOS/moodle-mod_questionnaire/releases/tag/v5.0.2) and the [Moodle plugins database](https://moodle.org/plugins/mod_questionnaire/5.0.2-build-2026040400/41075).

This will be the last release using this codebase. Future releases will utilize the new codebase [currently in development](https://github.com/orgs/PoetOS/projects/1/views/1?filterQuery=).
If you can support this project,[please consider it](https://github.com/sponsors/mchurchward).
